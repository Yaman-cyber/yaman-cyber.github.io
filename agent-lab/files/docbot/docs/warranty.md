# Warranty

All electronics and appliances come with a **2-year warranty** from the delivery date. Furniture has a **1-year warranty** against manufacturing defects.

## What the warranty covers
- Manufacturing defects
- Parts that stop working under normal use

## What it does not cover
- Accidental damage, water damage, or misuse
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, fading)
- Repairs done by anyone other than an authorized service center

## How to claim
Email warranty@acme-home.example with your order number, a short description and a photo or video of the problem. We reply within 2 business days.
