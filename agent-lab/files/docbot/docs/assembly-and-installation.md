# Assembly & Installation

## Furniture assembly
- **Free** for furniture orders over **AED 1,000** delivered to Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah.
- Otherwise it costs **AED 99 per item**.
- Beds, wardrobes and dining tables are always assembled by our team. Small items (chairs, side tables, shelves) come with tools and instructions.

## Appliance installation
| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Washing machine or dishwasher | AED 149 |
| Built-in oven or hob | AED 249 |
| TV wall mounting (bracket included, TVs up to 65 inches) | AED 199 |
| Split AC unit | Not offered; we recommend a licensed AC contractor |

## Booking a slot
After delivery, open **My Orders → Book assembly** and pick a time. Slots run from **9:00 to 21:00, Monday to Saturday**. You can move a booking free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts.

## Old appliance removal
We take your old appliance away **for free** when we install a new one of the same type, for example an old washing machine when we install a new washing machine.

## Safety
Our technicians carry an Acme Home ID card and never ask for cash. All services are paid online.
