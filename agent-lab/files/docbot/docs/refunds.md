# Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-01 · Applies to all orders placed on the Acme Home online store.

## Refund window
You can request a refund within **30 days** of the delivery date. After 30 days we can offer store credit only.

## How to request a refund
1. Open **My Orders** and choose the order (for example `ORD-10023`).
2. Select **Request refund** and pick a reason.
3. Ship the item back using the prepaid label within 7 days.

## When the money arrives
Once the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days**. Bank transfers to UAE banks may take 2 extra days.

## Items that cannot be refunded
- Gift cards
- Personalized or engraved items
- Opened mattresses and pillows (hygiene reasons)

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact support within 48 hours with a photo. We cover the return shipping and send a replacement or a full refund, your choice.
