# Privacy & Your Data

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## What we keep
- **Account details**: your name, email, phone number and delivery addresses.
- **Order history**: what you bought, when, and where it was delivered.
- **Chats with DocBot**: kept for **90 days**, then deleted automatically.
- **DocBot memories**: short facts DocBot saves to help you next time, such as your preferred language or your usual delivery city. They stay until you delete them.

## See and delete what DocBot remembers
Go to **Account → Privacy → What DocBot remembers**. You can delete one memory or all of them. DocBot stops using a memory as soon as you delete it.

## Delete your account
Email privacy@acme-home.example from the address on your account. We delete your account and personal data within **30 days**. We keep invoices for 5 years because UAE tax law requires it.

## Payments
Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

## What we never do
- We never sell your personal data.
- We do not use your chats or memories to train AI models.

## Where your data lives
All customer data is stored in data centres inside the UAE.
