# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery times (UAE)
| Area | Standard | Express |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah | 2–3 business days | Next day |
| Other emirates | 3–5 business days | 2 business days |

## Shipping fees
- Standard shipping is **free** on orders over **AED 200**. Below that, it costs AED 15.
- Express shipping costs **AED 35** on any order.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships. Order numbers start with `ORD-`, invoices with `INV-`.

## International shipping
We currently ship to Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar. Delivery takes 5–8 business days, and customs fees are paid by the customer.
