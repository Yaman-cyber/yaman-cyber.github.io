# Frequently Asked Questions

**What are your support hours?**
Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00 UAE time. Live chat is available every day from 9:00 to 22:00.

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Yes, within 1 hour of placing it, from My Orders. After that, the order is already being packed.

**Do you offer installment payments?**
Yes, on orders over AED 500 you can split the payment into 4 interest-free installments.

**Where is your showroom?**
Our showroom is in Khalifa City A, Abu Dhabi. Opening hours are 10:00 to 22:00 every day.

**Do you have a loyalty program?**
Acme Points: you earn 1 point per AED 10 spent. 100 points = AED 10 off your next order.
