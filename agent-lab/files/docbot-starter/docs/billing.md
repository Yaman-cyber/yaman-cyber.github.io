# Invoices & Payments

Last updated: 2026-09-01 · Applies to all orders placed on the Acme Home online store.

## Your invoice
We email your invoice as soon as your payment goes through. Invoice numbers start with `INV-` (for example `INV-20931`), and order numbers start with `ORD-`. You can also download any invoice from **My Orders → Invoice**.

## VAT
All prices on our website already include **5% VAT**. Your invoice shows the VAT amount separately, together with our tax registration number (TRN).

## Payment methods
- Visa and Mastercard credit or debit cards
- Apple Pay
- Cash on delivery: UAE addresses only, for orders up to **AED 2,000**, with a AED 10 handling fee
- Installments: on orders over **AED 500** you can split the payment into **4 interest-free installments** at checkout

International orders (Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar) can only be paid by card or Apple Pay. Cards from outside the UAE are charged in AED; your bank may add a currency conversion fee.

## Business invoices
Need your company name or TRN on the invoice? Email billing@acme-home.example within **30 days** of the purchase with the invoice number and your company details. We send a corrected invoice within 3 business days.

## Payment failed, but the money left my account
If checkout shows an error but your bank shows a charge, it is usually a temporary hold. Your bank releases it automatically within **7 business days**. If it is still there after that, contact billing@acme-home.example with your bank statement.
